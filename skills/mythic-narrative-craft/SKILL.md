# Mythic Narrative Craft & Classical Game Writing Standard

This standard codifies the narrative architecture, voice registers, mythic naming lexicons, dramatic techniques, and satirical parameters governing cinematic scripts, mission briefings, cutscenes, and unit voice lines across the *JSLC and Conquer* engine.

---

## 1. Core Mandate & Thematic Touchstones

### 1.1 The Battlestar Galactica (2004) Touchstone
The overarching creative axiom of this project is:
> **"If it played in Battlestar Galactica, it plays here."**

Game narrative must reject superficial heroic bravado in favor of procedural grit, existential fatigue, moral claustrophobia, and the cold weight of command. Key dramatic elements derived from this touchstone include:
1. **Mythic Resonance Over Military Hardware:** Nuclear-hardened warships, stealth strike aircraft, and laser apertures are christened not with generic sci-fi jargon, but with names carrying three thousand years of scriptural and mythological weight (*Merkava*, *Maccabee*, *Aegis*, *Scylla*, *Archangel*, *Battlestar Zion*).
2. **Exodus and Survival Over Imperial Triumphalism:** The driving impulse of the primary faction is not global conquest, but the fierce, unsentimental preservation of an encircled people. Commanders operate under the doctrine: *"We would rather stay alive than be loved; yet without logistics, we cannot stay alive."*
3. **The Enemy Among the Innocent:** Paranoia defines the operational theater. Combatants embed within civilian clinics, residential high-rises, and humanitarian convoys. Tactical decisions carry the constant hazard of catastrophic collateral damage and weaponized media fallout.
4. **Procedural Authenticity Meeting Mythic Dread:** Dialogue balances crisp naval and aerospace protocol (telemetry bearings, fuel states, EMCON locks, weapons release authorization) against biblical gravity and prophetic omens.
5. **The Cold Tribunal:** Warfare does not conclude with a victory parade, but with legal adjudications, evidentiary dossiers, and tribunal hearings conducted in impartial polar isolation.

## 2. Voice & Register Specifications

Dialogue and narrative prose must strictly adhere to five distinct registers. Tone drift between factions or violating register constraints breaks dramatic immersion.

### 2.1 JSLC (Jerusalem Command / JSL Corps)
- **Tone:** Weary, authoritative, biblically grounded, operationally precise. Think Commander William Adama in CIC during a DRADIS swarm countdown, layered with Tanakhic resonance.
- **Linguistic Rules:**
  - Sentences are declarative, imperative, and economically phrased.
  - Technical operational parameters follow ~80% ASD-STE100 guidelines (active voice, maximum 20 words for instructions, explicit physical units).
  - Hardware and operations reference Hebrew prophetic scripture, the Judges, the Maccabean revolt, or Kabbalistic emanations.
  - Avoid swagger, bloodlust, or triumphalist cheers. Success is greeted with grim relief; losses are acknowledged with ritual solemnity (*"May their memory be a shield"*).
- **Dialogue Anchor:**
  > *"CIC to Strike Lead. The Zagros battery has illuminated the flagship. The Philistine rockets are fueled. If the strait falls, the diaspora starves. Deliver the ordnance. Bring the airframes home."*

### 2.2 AoI (Axis of Intifada / The Network)
- **Tone:** Asymmetric zealotry fused with ruthless media cynicism and dark commercial absurdity. They know the camera is their most lethal weapon system.
- **Linguistic Rules:**
  - Public broadcasts project fiery revolutionary rhetoric and victimhood framing.
  - Tactical chatter is urgent, gritty, and street-level, interspersed with unshielded analog radio squelch.
  - Satirical contrast: juxtapose grand revolutionary declarations with cheap commercial consumerism (Temu parcel drops, AliExpress drone kits, Ms. Rachel educational songs echoing across bunker excavations).
  - Never caricature faith or ethnicity. The satire targets the cynical apparatus of authoritarian regime-backed proxy networks and the Western audiences they manipulate.
- **Dialogue Anchor:**
  > *"Keep the broadcast live from Ward 3! The moment their laser breaches the outer revetment, upload the stream to the London channels. The students will have the bridges blocked before dawn."*

### 2.3 The Directorate (The Penguins / PDA)
- **Tone:** Classical Roman senatorial stoicism, archaic Atlantean-Protoss detachment, and fierce, maternal protectiveness over their newborn young.
- **Linguistic Rules:**
  - Formal Latinate cadence, grand sentence architecture, high classical diction (Ciceronian or Thucydidean).
  - Speech is unhurried, measured, and devoid of colloquialisms or slang.
  - Absolute ethical red line: the gentle worker elephants and fluffy yellow chickies must be preserved at all costs. Any threat to the nursery crèches converts cool senatorial calm into cold, surgical psionic fury.
- **Dialogue Anchor:**
  > *"We have watched empires of dust wash against the ice shelves for ten millennia. Your treaties are etched in vapor. Present the cargo manifests to the praetor, human. And withdraw your engines from the crèche."*

### 2.4 International Lawfare & The Special Rapporteur (Francesca Albanese / ICC / UN)
- **Tone:** Sanctimonious, procedural, legalistic, passive-aggressive. The voice of weaponized international bureaucracy.
- **Linguistic Rules:**
  - Dense administrative jargon (proportionality thresholds, Rome Statute clauses, preliminary injunctions, Chapter VII notices).
  - Demands immediate evidentiary declassification within unrealistic time limits while turning a blind eye to asymmetric insurgent fortifications.
  - Never raises its voice; wields supply embargos and port closures like bureaucratic guillotine blades.
- **Dialogue Anchor:**
  > *"COMMUNIQUÉ FROM GENEVA: Kinetic discharge within grid square 14-Hotel is logged as disproportionate under Additional Protocol I, Article 57. Submit verified ISR telemetry within forty-five seconds, or allied bunkering permits in Djibouti will be revoked."*

### 2.5 Media Pundits & Transactional Patrons
- **Tone:** Cable news sensationalism, algorithmic hyperbole, transactional loyalty.
- **Linguistic Rules:**
  - High-tempo, buzzword-heavy rhetoric designed for TikTok algorithms and primetime outrage farming.
  - Loyalty is bought and sold on the influence market; rhetoric immediately pivots depending on who holds the contract.

## 4. Battlestar Galactica Narrative Architecture & Techniques

To achieve the authenticity of *Battlestar Galactica*, mission scripts and dialogue must employ five core structural techniques:

### 4.1 Technique 1: Cold Open with Telemetry Countdown
Every briefing or cutscene begins *in media res* with procedural telemetry, radar tracking (DRADIS/GMTI), and a ticking clock. Characters do not explain backstory; they react to imminent contact.
- **Structural Blueprint:**
  1. *Timestamp & Grid:* Greenwich Mean Time, coordinates, atmospheric weather tier.
  2. *Sensor Contact:* GMTI velocity vectors, radar return strength, acoustic signature.
  3. *Ticking Clock:* Time to weapon impact, bingo fuel, or orbital window closing.
  4. *The Command Commandment:* A decisive, unsentimental order issued under severe constraint.

### 4.2 Technique 2: The Moral Crucible (Command Under Scarcity)
In every operation, the commander must make a choice between two irreconcilable priorities:
- *Military Mission vs. Political Legitimacy:* Destroy an enemy radar emitter embedded in a hospital schoolyard, or preserve PR and risk losing a cruiser.
- *Force Preservation vs. Legal Scrutiny:* Authorize precision kinetic saturation now to save a pinned infantry squad, or wait forty seconds for ISR drone declassification to satisfy Geneva inspectors.
- *Logistical Trade-off:* PR collapses manifest not as artificial weapon jams, but as real-world logistical choking: delayed ammo convoys, closed supply berths, and activist blockades at base gates.

### 4.3 Technique 3: Enemies in Plain Sight (Civilians & Shields)
Echoing the paranoia of Cylon sleeper agents, the enemy exploits civilian space:
- Hijacked white UN food convoys siphoning armaments.
- Construction crews singing preschool songs while pouring bunker concrete.
- Improvised unshielded EMPs launched in urban alleys that knock out friendly and civilian electronics alike.

### 4.4 Technique 4: Combat Information Center (CIC) Cadence
Military radio traffic follows an authentic, layered call-and-response protocol:
1. *Speaker Identification:* "Adir Actual to Archangel..."
2. *Brevity Codes:* *Vampire* (hostile anti-ship missile), *Winchester* (out of ammunition), *Paveway* (laser guide locked), *Splash* (impact confirmed), *Angels* (altitude in thousands of feet), *EMCON Alpha* (total radio silence).
3. *Overlapping Telemetry:* Tactical officers read coordinates quietly in the background while commanders deliberate.

### 4.5 Technique 5: Prophetic Omens vs. Ballistic Physics
Juxtapose ancient scriptural prophecies with hard orbital telemetry. The characters believe in covenant, history, and destiny, but they know an unguided rocket obeys Newton, not prayers.

## 6. Satirical Guardrails & Ethical Boundaries

Per `bible/v0/LANGUAGE_SPEC.md`, satire in *JSLC and Conquer* is subject to strict, non-negotiable boundaries:

1. **Institutional Absurdity, Not Ethnic Stereotyping:**
   - The satire lampoons the hypocrisy of international organizations, the cynicism of state-sponsored proxy networks, the absurdity of corporate military sponsorships (Wendy's combat rations, Temu munitions drops), and the gullibility of Western social media audiences.
   - Never utilize ethnic, cultural, or religious slurs, tropes, or stereotypes. The AoI is strictly portrayed as a ruthless non-state militant network, never as a religious faith or an ethnic people.
2. **Logistical, Never Moral PR Enforcement:**
   - In accordance with user directive: *"we'd rather stay alive than be loved... so the pursuit of PR is in service of logistics."*
   - Bad PR triggers export holds, factory delays, funding freezes, and protester gate blockades. It **never** causes magical weapon jams, forced surrenders, or moral lectures from the game engine. Guns always shoot.
3. **Restraint in Tribunal Depictions:**
   - Battlefield tribunal dossiers and findings focus on forensic, institutional evidence (illicit weapon shipments, forged UN credentials, embezzled humanitarian concrete).
   - Graphic violence, anatomical gore, and child-related tribunal findings are strictly barred from player-facing UI and narrative text.
4. **Faction Name Locks:**
   - Factions are strictly **JSLC**, **AoI**, and **Penguins** (The Directorate). Any attempt to alter these names violates core project directives.

## 7. Narrative Audit & Verification Questions

Before submitting any narrative script, briefing, cutscene, or unit voice line, verify compliance against these ten audit criteria:

1. **The BSG Test:** Does this dialogue sound like it belongs in the CIC or flight deck of *Battlestar Galactica* during an existential crisis? Is it free of unearned quips and cartoon bravado?
2. **The Lexicon Sourcing:** Are the codenames drawn from verified Hebrew, Roman, or Greek classical sources with authentic historical or scriptural meanings?
3. **ASD-STE100 Check:** In technical briefings and UI text, are procedural instructions under 20 words, written in the active imperative, and free of vague synonyms?
4. **Logistical PR Check:** Does the narrative present Public Legitimacy as a physical logistical lifeline (shipping lanes, funding, supply blockades) rather than an abstract moral score?
5. **Satire Target Verification:** Is the target of the satire an institution, bureaucracy, propaganda campaign, or corporate absurdity, rather than an ethnic or religious identity?
6. **Faction Separation:** Does each faction speak strictly in its assigned register (JSLC = biblical/military grit; AoI = cynical zealotry/scrap tech; Directorate = classical Roman stoicism)?
7. **The Sacred Chickie Axiom:** Does Directorate narrative faithfully protect the gentle worker elephants and fluffy yellow chickies as non-negotiable moral centers?
8. **Real-World Metric Scale:** Do briefings and dialogue respect true physical scale (e.g. 333-metre supercarriers, 150-metre Sea Dragon, 1-metre fording limits)?
9. **Metric Weather Integration:** Does dialogue acknowledge the current METOC atmospheric conditions (e.g., laser thermal blooming in rain, mud traction loss)?
10. **The Operator Approval Stamp:** Is every newly proposed mission, character, or cutscene explicitly marked `[PROPOSED]` awaiting operator sign-off?
