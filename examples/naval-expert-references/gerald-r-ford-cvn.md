## 1. Identity, Variants & Hull Distinctions

- **CVN-78 USS *Gerald R. Ford*:** Commissioned 2017. Unique Dual Band Radar (DBR: AN/SPY-3 X-band MFR + AN/SPY-4 S-band VSR). Fitted with 4 EMALS catapults, 3 AAG water-twisters, 11 AWE elevators, and 2 Bechtel A1B reactors (~250 MWe; ~260k–280k shp, 4 shafts).
- **CVN-79+ (*John F. Kennedy*, *Enterprise* CVN-80, *Doris Miller* CVN-81):** DBR replaced by 3 fixed planar faces of **AN/SPY-6(V)3 EASR** ([DVIDS 7324023](https://www.dvidshub.net/image/7324023/kennedy-array-lift)) paired with rotating AN/SPQ-9B. Island reshaped; sponsons revised.
- **Distinctions vs *Nimitz* Class (CVN-68..77):**
  - **Island:** Sited **140 ft (42.7 m) aft** and **3 ft (0.91 m) outboard**; footprint ~130 ft vs ~180 ft.
  - **Elevators:** **3 deck-edge aircraft elevators** (2 stbd, 1 port aft) vs 4 on *Nimitz*.
  - **Catapults/Arresting:** 4 EMALS linear induction motors vs steam C-13; 3 AAG engines vs 4 Mk 7 Mod 3.
  - **Weapons Flow:** 11 AWE elevators route ordnance to 02 Level and flight deck, bypassing hangar floor.
  - **Hangar Bays:** **2 continuous bays** with 1 division door (Frame ~155) vs 3 bays with 2 doors.

## 5. Common 3D Modelling Errors

1. **Spinning Phased Arrays:** Animating SPY-3/SPY-4 DBR or SPY-6 EASR faces rotating. They are stationary planar arrays.
2. **Conflating Radar Suites:** Putting SPY-6 EASR on CVN-78 or SPY-3/4 DBR on CVN-79+. CVN-78 is DBR-only; CVN-79+ is EASR-only.
3. **Spinning SATCOM Shells:** Rotating outer radome enclosures. Outer shells are static; internal feeds track.
4. **Propeller Shaft Axis:** Rotating screws around global $Z$ or $Y$ rather than the actual canted propulsion shaft line.
5. **Predecessor Door Timing:** Applying *Nimitz* (CVN-69) 42 s door close time as a certified Ford specification. Ford timing is `unresolved`.
6. **Elevator / Wire Counts:** Modeling 4 aircraft elevators instead of 3; modeling 4 arresting wires instead of 3 AAG pendants.
7. **Island Position:** Placing island midships as on *Nimitz*. Ford island is **140 ft aft** and **3 ft outboard**.
8. **Inventing Primary Guns:** Adding 5-inch or 76mm naval gun turrets (CVN-78 has zero guns larger than 25mm).
9. **Tumblehome Hull:** Modeling inward tumblehome. Ford features extreme outward flare and overhangs.
