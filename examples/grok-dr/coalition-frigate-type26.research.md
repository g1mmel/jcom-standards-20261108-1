## Contradictions versus the base

Printed, not averaged:

- Displacement. Base: full load 8,000 t. Public pages: 6,900 tonnes on the Royal Navy equipment page, Naval Technology, and the Navy Lookout guide; 8,000 tonnes on the Royal Navy news item of 4 July 2025 and in the Navy Lookout mission-bay article, without the words "full load".
- Length. 149.9 m on Naval Technology and Navy Lookout; 149 m on the Royal Navy equipment page; 150 m in the 4 July 2025 Royal Navy news sentence. Base 149.900 m adds millimetre precision.
- Sea Ceptor. Royal Navy: 12 cells, quad-packed, 48 missiles. Navy Lookout: two 24-cell modules, 48 missiles. Base "48-cell" matches only Navy Lookout.

Does not hold:

- LBP 140 m and 142 m, waterline beam 19.5 m and 19.0 m, keel -6.5 m and -7.0 m, air draft 35.000 m to a TACAN apex and 38.5 m. Draft and masthead air draft are not public. The only related height is Navy Lookout's about 35 m to the Artisan antenna.
- Hull angles and the 11-station tables. Modelled, not public drawings.
- Controllable-pitch propellers. Navy Lookout says fixed propellers. That "fixed" statement is single-source, and it still contradicts the base.
- Rudders at plus or minus 35 degrees, Artisan at 30 rpm, navigation radar at 24 rpm, door slide of 5.0 m, and SATCOM at X = plus or minus 10.5 m. Not public.
- Coalition master well-deck ramp. UK Defence Journal's extract says the adopted design replaced an earlier stern well deck with a mission bay. Navy Lookout describes side doors.
- Coalition master box launcher at 30 degrees and a 32-cell midships bank. Not found. Public launchers are the disputed Sea Ceptor cells plus 24 Mk 41 cells.
- Flight-deck colour #00FF00. Present in the mesh audit as a game marking, not as an RN publication used here.
