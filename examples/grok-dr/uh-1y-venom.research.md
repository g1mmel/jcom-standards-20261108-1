## Numbers that failed the two-source test

- Current length 58 ft 4 in (17.78 m) is NAVAIR, the Navy fact file (same family), and Wikipedia. It is not labeled fuselage or rotors-turning. Archived NAVAIR prints fuselage 44 feet and overall 58 feet, with no metric on that page.
- Height 14 ft 7 in is agreed by current NAVAIR and Wikipedia. NAVAIR prints 4.5 m. Wikipedia prints 4.45 m. Archived NAVAIR prints 17 feet.
- Rotor 48 ft 10 in (14.88 m) is the UH-1Y NAVAIR/Navy/Wikipedia figure. Bell kiosk prints 48' with no inches and no metric. AH-1Z on the Navy fact file is 48 ft (14.6 m) and is a different aircraft.
- Tail-rotor diameter: not public.
- Empty weight: 11,840 lb (NAVAIR, 2022 plan, Wikipedia, with 5,370 kg versus 5,371 kg); 11,839 lb (brochure and 2018 NAVAIR); 11,700 lb (2015 plan, search extract).
- MTOW 18,500 lb is agreed. Kilograms 8,390 versus 8,391.
- Engine ratings: 1,800 shp (NAVAIR/Bell) versus 1,828 shp for 2 min 30 sec and 1,546 hp continuous (Wikipedia). Not collapsed.
- Fuel: 388 gal is Bell product only. 2,584 lb is brochure and 2018 NAVAIR. 77 gal auxiliary is the 2022 plan only. Not converted into each other.
- 16,700 lb basic design gross weight, sink speeds, and the 45 kt fold-wind limit: Bell brochure only.
- 21-inch (530 mm) fuselage extension and rotor area 1,808 sq ft: Wikipedia only. The printed 530 mm was not replaced by a recalculation.
- Hot-day payload 3,079 lb and the 10,000 ft operational altitude: 2018 NAVAIR only.
- Climb 2,520 ft/min, Vne 198 kn, and several Wikipedia performance cells: that page, with the 2001 brochure echoing 164 and 158 kts only.
- Sideward/rearward 45 KIAS and the g envelope: Bell product page.
- Fold hinge angle, pivot XYZ, tail-fold travel, store-station coordinates, and sensor coordinates: not public.
- Stub-wing span: not applicable. The UH-1Y source set describes two external stations, not AH-1Z stub wings.

## Game conflicts

- lengthM 17.78 agrees with current NAVAIR / Navy fact file / Wikipedia 58 ft 4 in. It does not match archived NAVAIR overall length of 58 feet, and the archived fuselage length of 44 feet is a different measure the game does not store.
- widthM 14.88 is the NAVAIR UH-1Y rotor diameter, not a borrowed AH-1Z 14.6 m figure. The game source string calls it an H-1 representative. Bell's kiosk says 48 feet and omits the 10 inches, so the game matches NAVAIR and not the kiosk wording.
- heightM 4.5 matches the metre NAVAIR prints for 14 ft 7 in. It conflicts with Wikipedia's 4.45 m conversion of those inches and with archived NAVAIR height of 17 feet.
- massTonnes null. 18,500 lb is public, and the kilogram rounding disagrees by 1 kg, so no tonne value was written into the game check as a correction.
